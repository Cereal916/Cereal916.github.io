HOUSE OF PIPS: A DOMINO ROGUELIKE
Media fact sheet

House of Pips is a turn-based domino roguelike where you draft rule-bending perks, turn clever tile placements into big scoring combos, and take on rivals solo or across the table online.

Learn the basics at Nana's table, then make the rules your own. Swap tiles, change their pips, or turn blanks into wildcards. Draft perks that work together and unlock new options through the skill tree. Face computer rivals, challenge another player online, or work through 20 untimed solo puzzles.
