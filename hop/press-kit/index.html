About the game

Learn the basics at Nana's table, then make the rules your own. Swap tiles, change their pips, or turn blanks into wildcards. Draft perks that work together and unlock new options through the skill tree. Face computer rivals, challenge another player online, or work through 20 untimed solo puzzles.

Creator quick start

Start at Nana's table to learn how the open ends score. Play a solo run through your first perk draft and show how the next hand plays differently. If you are recording with another creator, try an online match. The free Steam demo is available if you want to try the game before requesting a key.
