# Supply notes

Hand-written "How it works" text for every supply, keyed by catalogue ID. The
wiki builder fails when a supply is missing here or a section names an unknown
supply.

## table_pass

Use it on your turn when **you have no legal play** and the boneyard still has
bones. Instead of drawing, you **skip the draw and your turn passes** to Rival.

- It is only offered when a draw would otherwise be required.
- It counts as a trip to the boneyard for perks: your play streaks reset and
  {{perk:draw}} is ready for your next play.
- Handy when the boneyard is full of bones you don't want, or when
  {{perk:leak}} or {{perk:greedy}} would make a draw expensive.

## xp_boost_1

Activate it from the Inventory between matches. Your **next match earns 25%
more XP** from every source.

- Activating another boost adds its matches to the queue instead of raising the
  bonus, so two Quick Study 1s cover two matches.
- A queued boost is used by the next match you start in a mode that allows
  supplies. You can't activate a boost while a match is in progress or saved.

## xp_boost_3

Same as Quick Study 1, but it covers your **next three matches** with +25% XP.

## xp_boost_5

Same as Quick Study 1, but it covers your **next five matches** with +25% XP.

## income_boost_1

Activate it from the Inventory between matches. Your **next match earns 50%
more Bone Bucks**.

- Like Quick Study, extra activations add matches to the queue rather than
  raising the bonus.

## income_boost_3

Same as Golden Ledger I, but it covers your **next three matches**.

## income_boost_5

Same as Golden Ledger I, but it covers your **next five matches**.

## blank_check

On your turn, choose one of your bones with a blank and pick a number from 1 to
6: **every blank half on that bone becomes that number**. On the double blank
both halves change, so 0-0 can become 6-6.

- The bone keeps its new numbers for placement and scoring, and the game picks
  it up ready to play.
- It needs a blank bone in your hand.

## boneyard_lantern

Use it in the hand setup screen before a hand. On your **next trip to the
boneyard**, one random face-down bone is shown face-up before you choose.

- If you don't visit the boneyard that hand, the light waits for your next
  visit in the same match.
- The {{perk:boneyard_peek}} perk does the same on every visit.

## rail_pass

When you draw a single bone that **cannot be played** and the boneyard still has
another bone, the game offers to spend a Rail Pass. You then **pick a second
face-down bone, both are turned over, and you keep the one you want**.

- It is only offered on a plain one-bone draw.
- If you decline, the draw finishes normally.

## pip_eraser

On your turn, choose an open end. On your **next play**, that end counts **one
pip less** when the table adds up the open ends. The bone on the table doesn't
change.

- Use it to turn a total such as 16 into a scoring 15.
- The erased end has to still be open after your play. If you play on that end
  it is covered, nothing is erased, and the Pip Eraser is still spent.

:::example
The open ends are 6, 4 and 3. You erase the 6, then play 3-6 on the open 3. The
ends are now 6, 4 and 6, which is 16, but the erased 6 counts as 5: the play
scores **15**.
:::

## hammer_wax

Use it in the hand setup screen. If you **win that hand and pass the hammer** to
Rival, your compensation draft has **better rarity odds**: commons become much
less likely and every higher rarity more likely.

- It is spent when you use it. If you lose the hand, or win and keep the
  hammer, it is wasted.
- See {{page:matches}} for the hammer and the compensation draft.

## dealers_dare

Use it in the hand setup screen and **stake one of your supplies**: a supply you
own at least one of, that isn't at its holding limit, and isn't another
Dealer's Dare.

- **Win the hand:** you get **two copies** of the staked supply, up to its
  holding limit. That's one more than you staked.
- **Lose the hand:** the staked copy is gone.
- Dealer's Dare itself is used up either way.
