The skill tree holds your permanent career upgrades. Unlike perks, skills stay
with you from match to match. Every career level you gain gives you **one
skill point**, and each point buys one rank of one skill. The tree has
{{n:career.skillRankCapacity}} ranks in total across three tabs:

{{skill-branches}}

How the tree works:

- A skill unlocks once the skill above it reaches the rank shown on the line
  between them (a small **R2** tag means rank 2 is needed). The top skill on
  each tab is available straight away.
- Skills with several ranks get stronger with each point you spend.
- **Respec** refunds every point you spent for {{n:career.respecCost}} Bone Bucks,
  or for free if you have a free respec from the Slap Track.
- Old School turns skills off. Online matches use a separate skill build; see
  {{page:online}}.
- Skills are not perks: they don't count as perk bonuses for
  {{perk:midas}}, {{perk:echo}} or {{perk:tax}}.
