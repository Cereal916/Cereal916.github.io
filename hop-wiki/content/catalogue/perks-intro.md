Perks are the cards you draft during a match. Each one bends the rules of the
table for the rest of that match: extra points, peeks at hidden bones, swaps,
wild plays and more. Maluses are the red cards that bend the rules against
you. Every perk below links to a page with its exact behaviour, how copies
stack and a worked example where the maths matters.

{{rarity-legend}}

Some quick rules that apply to every perk:

- **Copies stack.** Drafting the same perk twice usually doubles its effect or
  its uses. Each perk page says exactly how its copies combine.
- **A scoring play** is any play after which the open ends add up to a
  positive multiple of five. Bonus perks only add points to scoring plays;
  they never turn a non-scoring play into one. See {{page:scoring}} for the
  order in which bonuses, multipliers and maluses are applied.
- **Uses refill every hand.** Perks with limited uses (marked *Uses* on their
  page) get them back at the start of each hand. The card text says "per
  round"; a round and a hand are the same thing.
- **Perks last for the match.** Everything you draft is gone when the match
  ends. Unlocking a perk in the Perks compendium only adds it to the cards
  drafts can offer you.
- **Rival drafts the same cards.** A perk in Rival's hands has the same effect
  it would have for you.
