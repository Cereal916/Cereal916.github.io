Supplies are consumable items you keep in your Inventory between matches. Buy
them in the **Supply Shop** with Bone Bucks, find them in **Bone Boxes**, or
claim them on the **Slap Track**. Each one is used up when you spend it.

There are three kinds:

- **Table supplies** are used during a match: on your turn, at the
  boneyard, or in the hand setup screen that opens before each hand.
- **XP boosts** (Quick Study) and **Bone Bucks boosts** (Golden Ledger) are
  activated from the Inventory between matches and cover your next few
  matches.

Supplies are switched off in Old School, in Puzzles, and in any Custom match
with the Old School modifier. Each supply has a limit on how many you can hold;
the shop stops selling it once you reach the limit, and Bone Boxes refund extra
copies as Bone Bucks. See {{page:collections}} for the shop, Bone Boxes and the
Slap Track.
