# Game modes

The **Modes** screen chooses which ruleset the **Play** button starts. A new
career has Classic Table; every other mode unlocks by completing the career
trials printed on its card.

{{shot:modes}}

## The modes

{{modes-table}}

A few notes on the rules above:

- **Old School** is traditional dominoes: no perks, no skills and no supplies.
  It unlocks as soon as you finish Nana's tutorial.
- **All In** gives you every card in every draft, Malus included, so builds
  grow fast in both directions.
- **Quick Draw** deals five-bone hands, so hands end quickly. Its unlock trial
  "Call 15 Fivers" counts plays that score exactly 5 points.
- **The Long Haul** gives the loser of each hand two drafts in a row.
- **House of Malice** starts both players with two random maluses.
- **Legendary Table** offers three legendary perks in every draft, with no
  Malus. Its trials ask you to unlock every legendary perk first.
- **Custom** appears once you have won a match in every other mode on the
  list.
