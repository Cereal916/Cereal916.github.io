# Puzzles

Puzzles are hand-made solo challenges. Each one gives you a **fixed rack, a
fixed table and sometimes a few perks**, and asks you to meet a goal such as
"Play all 5 bones without scoring" or "Score at least 50 points in a single
play". There is no opponent and no boneyard, so every puzzle is pure planning.

{{shot:puzzles}}

## How puzzles work

- There are **20 puzzles in four sets of five**.
- The first five are open from the start. Every **four new solves** open the
  next five.
- You can try a puzzle as often as you like and replay solved ones.
- Puzzles don't autosave: if you leave an unfinished puzzle, it starts over
  next time.
- Puzzles don't use supplies and don't earn career XP.

{{shot:puzzle-board}}

## Rewards

- **Finishing a set** unlocks that set's domino skin (Arcane Magic, Skull and
  Crossbones, Galaxy and Jigsaw) and a player portrait.
- **Solving all twenty** earns the **Puzzle Master** achievement and an online
  placement effect.

## Tips

- Read the goal twice. "Without scoring" puzzles are about keeping the open
  ends **off** multiples of five.
- Count the open ends before every play, including after your next play.
- Remember that an open double counts both of its halves. See
  {{page:rules}}.
