# Rival and table styles

**Rival** is your opponent across the table in solo matches. He drafts perks
and maluses from the same cards you do, and his perks have the same effects as
yours. When Rival uses a perk, the table pauses on a
notice so you can see what happened; the ledger records it too.

## Skill levels

Rival sits down at one of three skill levels. In Custom mode you choose the
level.

{{rival-levels}}

## Table styles

Rival plays with a style: a plan for the table. Rival School has one lesson
for each style, and in Custom mode you can pick Rival's style.

{{rival-styles}}

## Rival School

{{shot:rival-school}}

Open **Tutorials** and choose **Enter Rival School** (it opens after Nana's
tutorial). Each lesson shows a real table position, asks you to choose
between two playable options, and explains why the style picks the one it
does. Completed lessons stay checked in your career, and finishing all seven
earns the **Know Your Rival** achievement.

## Rewards don't depend on difficulty

Rival's skill level never changes the XP or Bone Bucks you earn.
