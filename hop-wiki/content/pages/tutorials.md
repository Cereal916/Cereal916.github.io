# Tutorials

{{shot:tutorial-menu}}

## Nana's tutorial

Nana teaches the rules of her house in seventeen short steps:

- the bones, their pips and the hammer
- leading with a double, laying doubles crosswise and spinners
- matching an open end and rotating a bone onto a vertical branch
- scoring on multiples of five and counting an open double
- drawing from the boneyard, blocked hands and winning a hand

### Good to know

- **It is required.** Play, Puzzles and Multiplayer stay locked until you
  finish it once.
- Finishing it unlocks **Old School** and earns the **Nana Knows Best**
  achievement. Nana then sits down for a short Old School game with you, first
  to 75.
- Every step works with the mouse, the keyboard or a controller, and the
  on-screen prompts change to match.
- You can replay it from **Tutorials > Replay Nana** at any time.

## Rival School

Rival School opens after Nana's tutorial. Rival shows you seven table styles,
one lesson each: Score Hound, Table Controller, Lock Artist, Counterpuncher,
Domino Racer, Pip Shedder and Combo Crafter.

Each lesson sets up a real table, gives you two playable choices, and explains
which one the style prefers and why.

- Progress is saved, so you can take the lessons in any order and come back
  later.
- Finishing all seven earns the **Know Your Rival** achievement.
- See {{page:rivals}} for what each style wants.
