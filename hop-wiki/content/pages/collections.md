# Shop, Bone Boxes and cosmetics

## Supply Shop

The **Supply Shop** sells supplies for Bone Bucks. Each supply has a holding
limit, and the shop won't sell you more than that. Four supplies are never
sold: {{supply:blank_check}}, {{supply:rail_pass}}, {{supply:pip_eraser}} and
{{supply:hammer_wax}} only come from Bone Boxes and the Slap Track. Every
supply is described on the [Supplies](supplies.html) page.

{{shot:supply-shop}}

## Inventory

The **Inventory** holds what you own:

- **Supplies.** Table supplies are used from the match itself; XP and Bone
  Bucks boosts are activated here between matches.
- **Bone Boxes**, ready to open.

You can sort the Inventory by ownership, value, rarity, name or count.

## Bone Boxes

A Bone Box is a sealed crate of random supplies. Buy one in the shop, earn one
on the Slap Track, then open it from the Inventory. Each supply inside rolls
its rarity using the odds printed on the box, then picks a supply of that
rarity at random.

{{bone-boxes}}

If a supply would take you over its holding limit, you get a **Bone Bucks
refund** instead of the item: part of the box's price, and more for rarer
items.

{{shot:inventory-boxes}}

## The Slap Track

{{shot:slap-track}}

The **Slap Track** has one reward for each career level, up to level 30:
supplies, Bone Boxes and cosmetics. Claim them in order from the Slap Track
screen.

- A supply or Bone Box you can't hold any more pays its full Bone Bucks price
  instead.
- A cosmetic you already own is replaced by a {{supply:dealers_dare}}.
- After a Prestige the track starts again: levels 10 and 30 give a free skill
  respec, and the cosmetic levels hold new cosmetics.

## Cosmetics

Cosmetics change how the game looks and sounds. They never affect play. Choose
them from **Cosmetics** on the main menu.

{{shot:cosmetics-dominoes}}

- **Table skins**: the background felt behind the table. Classic Felt is the
  default; more come from the Slap Track.
- **Domino skins**: the design on the back of every bone.
  - Plain Ivory is the default.
  - The Alpha, Three Decades, Cyberpunk Circuit and Steampunk Gear come from
    the Slap Track.
  - Arcane Magic, Skull and Crossbones, Galaxy and Jigsaw are the rewards for
    the four puzzle sets. See {{page:puzzles}}.
  - **Seasonal skins** unlock for good when you open the game in their month:
    Valentine Heart in February, Halloween Night in October and Silver and
    Gold in December.
  - Pride skins come from the Slap Track after Prestige.
  - The Country Flag collection is a separate add-on.
- **Background music**: songs for the table. The House Theme is the default;
  more songs come from the Slap Track and Prestige. Settings has a shuffle
  option.
- **Avatars**: your player portrait, chosen from **Choose your avatar** on the
  main menu. More portraits come from the Slap Track, puzzle sets and online
  play.

Online play has its own cosmetic rewards: tile backs, edge finishes,
placement effects and badges. See {{page:online}}.
