# Matches, hands and drafts

A **match** is one game against Rival, from the first hammer draw until
someone reaches the target score. It is made of **hands** (the table also says
**rounds**; they are the same thing). Perks you draft last for the whole match
and are gone when it ends.

## How a match runs

1. **Hammer draw.** You and Rival each draw a face-down bone; the higher double
   takes the hammer and plays first.
2. **Opening drafts.** You choose your opening perk, then Rival chooses theirs.
   In House of Malice both players also start with two random maluses.
3. **Each hand:**
   1. Choose your bones for the hand (or take a random hand).
   2. The **hand setup** screen offers optional pre-hand actions.
   3. Play the hand. See {{page:rules}}.
   4. Read the **hand summary** and receipts.
   5. If you won, keep or pass the hammer. Then the drafts for the next hand
      happen.
4. **Match end.** The first player to reach the target while ahead wins. The
   final summary shows your match rewards.

## Hand setup: optional actions before a hand

{{shot:hand-setup}}

Before the first bone lands you can use:

- **Last Call** (free, once per match): mark one bone in your opening hand. If
  that bone is the **last one you play when you go domino** that hand, you win
  a jackpot of **750 XP and 750 Bone Bucks**. If you play the marked bone
  earlier, Last Call expires.
- {{supply:boneyard_lantern}}: light up one bone on your next boneyard trip.
- {{supply:hammer_wax}}: improve the compensation draft if you win and pass
  the hammer.
- {{supply:dealers_dare}}: stake a supply on winning this hand.

Then choose **Begin hand**.

## Drafts

Perk drafts are where your build comes from.

{{shot:draft}}

### Who drafts, and when

- **At the start of the match** you draft your opening perk, then Rival does.
- **After every hand, the loser drafts.** In The Long Haul (and with the Double
  Down modifier) the loser drafts twice in a row.
- **If you win a hand and pass the hammer** to Rival, you get a
  **compensation draft** before the loser's draft. Rival never passes the
  hammer.
- Old School has no drafts at all.

### What a draft offers

A standard draft shows **two perk cards and one Malus card**. You take **one**
card. Each Prestige rank adds one more perk card to your drafts (not in All
In). Perk cards only come from perks you have unlocked in the Perks
compendium; the Malus card can be any malus.

Why would you ever take the Malus? Usually you don't: you take a perk and the
Malus goes away. Two buttons and a skill change that:

- **Take & Redraw** (the Malus bargain): accept **only the Malus**, and the
  house deals you a **fresh offer**. Once per draft.
- **Take all**, from the {{skill:take_all}} skill: take **every card**,
  perks and Malus together.

Some modes change the draft:

- **All In** gives you every card automatically, Malus included.
- **Legendary Table** shows three legendary perks and no Malus; choose one.

### Changing the offer

- **Reroll** ({{skill:draft_rerolls}} skill): replace one perk card with a fresh
  one. One free reroll per rank in every draft.
- **Save** ({{skill:fortune_mortgage}} skill): reserve one perk card. It comes
  back as the first card of your next eligible draft, and you must take a
  different perk card now.
- **Fresh Cut** ({{perk:reroll}} perk): upgrade the lowest-rarity perk card by
  one rarity.
- {{skill:fate_echo}} (skill): a perk you take may echo as a temporary extra
  copy for the next hand.

### Draft odds

Common perks show up most often and legendary perks least. Several things raise
your odds of rarer cards: the Fortune skills {{skill:rarity_luck}},
{{skill:rare_focus}}, {{skill:legendary_touch}} and {{skill:lucky_recovery}},
and {{supply:hammer_wax}} on a compensation draft. In single-player, legendary
perks only appear once you have unlocked them. Online, every legendary perk the
match allows can appear for both players.

## The hammer choice

When you win a hand (and the match isn't over), you choose:

- **Keep the hammer**: you lead the next hand. Only Rival drafts.
- **Pass the hammer**: Rival leads the next hand, and you get a compensation
  draft before Rival's loser draft.

Leading is an advantage: the hammer holder sets the first open ends and plays
one bone ahead.

## The hand summary and receipts

{{shot:hand-win}}

After each hand the summary shows the score change and the hand award. Two
receipt pages follow:

- The **XP receipt** lists where your XP came from. See {{page:career}}.
- The **Bone Bucks receipt** lists every Bone Buck you earned.

{{shot:bucks-receipt}}

## The ledger

Open the **Ledger** at any time during a match (or press `L`). It lists every
play with its open-end total, each perk bonus and malus, every perk and supply
you or Rival used, and boneyard trips. If a score surprises you, the ledger
shows exactly where it came from.

{{shot:ledger}}

## Leaving a match

Your match is saved automatically. **Save and exit** in Settings returns you to
the menu, and **CONTINUE** on the main menu brings you back. See
{{page:saving}} for **Rage quit**.
