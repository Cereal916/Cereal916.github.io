# Controls, controllers and Steam Deck

You can play House of Pips with a mouse, a keyboard, a controller, or any mix of
them. On-screen prompts follow whatever you used last.

## Mouse

- **Click** a bone in your rack to pick it up, then click a glowing play
  location. You can also **drag** the bone there and let go.
- **Right-click** while holding a bone turns it 90 degrees, for the vertical
  branches of a spinner.
- **Hover** almost anything for a tooltip: perks, supplies, bones, scores and
  buttons.
- A label or name shortened with dots to fit, as tabs can be at the Extra
  large text size, shows its full text when you hover it.
- Buttons have flat backgrounds and colored borders. Hover over an enabled
  button to show its gold outline.
- Perks you can use and your table supplies sit left of the table as small
  buttons showing their icon and remaining uses, each column from the lowest
  rarity down. Hover one to read its name and what it does. An armed perk
  wears a gold ring.
- Click the **boneyard** when you have no legal play.
- Click a glowing **envelope** at the top to inspect a friend's multiplayer
  invitation. In its panel, **Esc** or controller **Back** chooses Later and
  keeps the invitation. During a table, the controller's action button
  (Xbox **X** / PlayStation **Square**) reaches the envelope with the other
  table actions.

While holding a bone, {{skill:pip_forecast}} adds compact projected-total
numbers beside the landing previews. A small legend in the status column shows
what the colors mean: white is the open-end total, pink is a total that
scores. Hover the legend for details. An armed placement perk shows its name
and remaining uses once in the status column, with a small marker on each
affected landing. Hover or focus a landing to inspect its outcome and any
required rotation. {{perk:pip_nudge}} keeps its **+1** or **−1** cue.

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `1` to `0` | Pick up the bone in that rack slot |
| Arrow keys | Cycle through the places the bone can go |
| `Enter` | Play the bone at the selected place |
| `R` | Rotate the bone you are holding |
| `Esc` | Cancel the current selection |
| `Space` | Draw from the boneyard |
| `L` | Open the match ledger |
| `Space` or `Enter` | Speed up draft reveals and hand summaries |
| `Enter` or `Esc` in a confirmation | Confirm or cancel it |
| Arrow keys, `Tab`, `Shift`+`Tab` | Move between menu buttons |
| `N` | Next song |
| `F11` | Toggle fullscreen |
| `F1` | Toggle menu narration |

## Controllers

- Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo Switch style controllers and the Steam Deck
  are supported. Button prompts show the right symbols for your controller.
- A button prompt that cannot do anything right now is grayed out. While you
  hold a bone, **Place** grays out whenever the bone does not fit the end you
  are aiming at as it is turned.
- Controls go through **Steam Input**, so you can remap them in Steam. Open
  **Settings > Access > Edit in Steam**. Remapped buttons show their new
  prompts in the game.
- Menus, matches and Nana's tutorial all work with a controller.
- Standalone controller hints have dark bordered panels so their symbols and
  labels stay readable over the table artwork.
- By default, PlayStation **Cross** or Xbox/Steam Deck **A** confirms the
  focused choice; **Circle** or **B** goes back or cancels. Page arrows use
  **L1/R1** or **LB/RB**; those buttons switch tabs in the skill tree.
- Cutscene **Pause/Resume** uses **Triangle/Y**. **Continue** uses **Cross/A**
  when its button appears. Rank Up dialogue waits for Continue on each line;
  **Enter** also continues it.
- During the opponent's turn you can inspect your rack. **Select** dims
  while the focused bone cannot be played.

- A committed boneyard draw must finish before you can return to your rack.
  **Rack locked** and its Back prompt are dimmed during the choice; choice
  prompts disappear while the bones slide into your hand.

- During a committed perk burn and reveal, **Cross/A** speeds it up. Return
  to supplies after the result settles.

## Steam Deck

House of Pips plays on Steam Deck, and Steam Cloud carries your career
between the Deck and your PC.

- The game is laid out for the Deck's 1280×800 screen and starts on the
  **Extra large** text size there, so small text stays readable. Docked to a
  1080p or larger screen it switches to **Standard** straight away. The Deck's
  screen and the docked screen each keep the size you choose under
  **Settings > Access > Text size**.
- On the Deck and other screens smaller than 1920×1080, the bones at the
  open ends, a spinner with an open side included, are drawn larger than
  the rest of the train, so their pips stay readable however long the
  train grows. The train makes room for them, so they never cover another
  bone or a drop target.
- **Large** drag targets in Settings make the small screen more comfortable.
