# Controls, controllers and Steam Deck

You can play House of Pips with a mouse, a keyboard, a controller, or any mix of
them. On-screen prompts follow whatever you used last.

## Mouse

- **Click** a bone in your rack to pick it up, then click a glowing play
  location. You can also **drag** the bone there and let go.
- **Right-click** while holding a bone turns it 90 degrees, for the vertical
  branches of a spinner.
- **Hover** almost anything for a tooltip: perks, supplies, bones, scores and
  buttons.
- Click the **boneyard** when you have no legal play.

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `1` to `0` | Pick up the bone in that rack slot |
| Arrow keys | Cycle through the places the bone can go |
| `Enter` | Play the bone at the selected place |
| `R` | Rotate the bone you are holding |
| `Esc` | Cancel the current selection |
| `Space` | Draw from the boneyard |
| `L` | Open the match ledger |
| `Space` or `Enter` | Speed up draft reveals and hand summaries |
| Arrow keys, `Tab`, `Shift`+`Tab` | Move between menu buttons |
| `N` | Next song |
| `F11` | Toggle fullscreen |
| `F1` | Toggle menu narration |

## Controllers

- Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo Switch style controllers and the Steam Deck
  are supported. Button prompts show the right symbols for your controller.
- Controls go through **Steam Input**, so you can remap them in Steam. Open
  **Settings > Accessibility > Edit in Steam**. Remapped buttons show their new
  prompts in the game.
- Menus, matches and Nana's tutorial all work with a controller.

## Steam Deck

House of Pips plays on Steam Deck, and Steam Cloud carries your career
between the Deck and your PC.

- The game is laid out for the Deck's 1280×800 screen, and text never drops
  below a readable minimum size there.
- The **Large** text size and **Large** drag targets in Settings make the
  small screen more comfortable.
