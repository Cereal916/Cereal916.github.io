# Achievements

House of Pips has {{n:counts.achievements}} achievements. The ones below are
visible from the start; the rest are hidden until you earn them, and this
wiki doesn't list them.
The gallery groups tutorials, match milestones, collections, modes, puzzles,
career and Rank Up, then hidden achievements. The Rank Up badges stay together
in rank order, and hidden badges keep their group after you earn them.

Scroll the gallery to see every badge. A hidden badge and its mode-checklist
label become visible only after you earn that specific achievement.

## Career ranks

**Another Round** marks your first rank up, which reaches Rank 2. The next
three milestones are **Third Time's the Charm** at Rank 3, **Four of a Kind**
at Rank 4 and **House Legend** at Rank 5. These require two, three and four
completed rank ups respectively.

These are career ranks earned through Rank Up, separate from your online
rank. See {{page:career}} for the rank rewards and resets.
Earlier reached ranks count when you launch. Opening or canceling
Rank Up earns nothing; completing it earns the badge once the new rank is saved.

## Checklists

Several achievements track a checklist. Open an achievement's **Details** to
see which entries you still need:

- **Well Supplied**: use each supply at least once. Buying it doesn't count.
- **Every Trick in the Book**: put every positive perk to use in a match.
- **Hard Knocks**: experience the effect of every malus.
- **Open House**: unlock every perk in the Perks compendium.
- **A Table for Every Taste**: win a match in every game mode. Some modes stay
  hidden on the checklist until you reach them.

## Progress and Steam

- Achievement progress is a lifetime record: it carries across rank ups.
- With Steam running, earned achievements also unlock on Steam, and the
  gallery shows how many players have earned each one.
- **Clean Slate** clears local achievement progress, but achievements already
  unlocked on Steam stay there.
