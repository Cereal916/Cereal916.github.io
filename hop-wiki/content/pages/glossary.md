# Glossary

- **Blocked hand**: A hand where nobody can play and the boneyard is empty. The lower pip total wins. See {{page:rules}}.
- **Bone**: A domino tile. The set has 28 bones, 0-0 to 6-6.
- **Bone Box**: A crate of random supplies, bought or earned and opened from the Inventory.
- **Bone Bucks**: The in-game currency, earned every hand and match. See {{page:career}}.
- **Boneyard**: The face-down pile of bones nobody holds. You draw from it when you can't play.
- **Branch**: A line of bones growing out of a spinner. The up and down arms are vertical branches.
- **Charge** or **use**: One activation of a perk with limited uses. Most refill every hand.
- **Clean rack**: A hand in which you never drew from the boneyard. Pays a Bone Bucks bonus.
- **Compensation draft**: The extra draft you get for passing the hammer after winning a hand.
- **Domino, going domino**: Playing your last bone, which wins the hand.
- **Double**: A bone with the same number on both halves, such as 5-5.
- **Draft**: Choosing one card from an offer of perks and a Malus. See {{page:matches}}.
- **Fiver**: A scoring play: the open ends add up to a multiple of five. Bigger ones are called Double Fiver, Triple Fiver and so on.
- **Hammer**: The right to play first in a hand. The hand winner takes it and may pass it.
- **Hand** or **round**: One deal of bones, played until someone goes domino or the hand is blocked. Perk cards that say "per round" mean per hand.
- **Hand award**: The loser's remaining pips, rounded up to a multiple of five, scored by the hand's winner.
- **Hand setup**: The screen before each hand where Last Call and pre-hand supplies are used.
- **Last Call**: Mark a bone before a hand; go domino with it as your final bone to win 750 XP and 750 Bone Bucks. Once per match.
- **Malus** (plural maluses): A red card that works against whoever holds it. One appears in every standard draft.
- **Match**: One game against Rival or an online opponent, played to a target score.
- **Open end**: An exposed number on the edge of the table that a bone can be played against. Their sum decides scoring.
- **Perk**: A card that changes the rules in your favour for the rest of the match.
- **Pip**: One dot on a bone. A bone's pip total is both halves added together.
- **Prestige**: Restarting your career climb at the level cap for permanent bonuses. See {{page:career}}.
- **Public bone**: A bone that has been revealed to both players, for example by {{perk:open_book}}, {{perk:pip_shift}} or {{perk:swap_rival}}.
- **Rack**: The bones in a player's hand.
- **Rarity**: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic or Legendary for perks and supplies; maluses are their own group: {{rarity:common}} {{rarity:uncommon}} {{rarity:rare}} {{rarity:epic}} {{rarity:legendary}} {{rarity:malus}}
- **Scoring play**: A play after which the open ends total a positive multiple of five.
- **Skill**: A permanent upgrade bought with skill points in the skill tree.
- **Slap Track**: The row of level-up rewards, one for each career level up to 30.
- **Spinner**: A double. Once both long sides are covered, its short sides open too.
- **Stack**: Holding more than one copy of the same perk. Most perks get stronger with each copy.
- **Supply**: A consumable item kept in your Inventory and used up when spent.
