# New player guide

## 1. Take Nana's tutorial

A brand-new career starts with most of the main menu locked: **Play**,
**Puzzles** and **Multiplayer** all say "Finish Nana's tutorial first."

Open **Tutorials** and play **Nana's tutorial**. In a few minutes she teaches
the whole table, from pips and the hammer to scoring, the boneyard and winning
a hand.

{{shot:tutorial-lesson}}

Finishing it unlocks the rest of the menu, and Nana invites you straight into
a short Old School game against her (first to 75). Completing that match,
win or lose, unlocks **Old School**. **Classic Table** stays selected for your
next game; choose Old School from **Modes** whenever you want to play it.
See {{page:tutorials}} for both tutorials.

## 2. Find your way around the menu

{{shot:main-menu}}

- **Play** starts a match in the mode shown on the button. A new career plays
  **Classic Table**, first to 150.
- **Puzzles** are hand-made solo challenges. See {{page:puzzles}}.
- **Multiplayer** plays a friend or a stranger through Steam. See
  {{page:online}}.
- **Tutorials** replays Nana's lesson and opens Rival School.
- **Modes** chooses which ruleset **Play** uses. See {{page:modes}}.
- **Perks** is the compendium where you unlock perks for your drafts with
  Bone Bucks.
- **Skill tree** spends the skill points you earn by levelling up.
- **Slap Track** pays one reward for every career level.
- **Career** shows your records and match history.
- **Supply Shop**, **Inventory**, **Cosmetics** and **Choose your avatar** hold
  the things you buy and earn. See {{page:collections}}.
- **Settings** has sound, display, language, accessibility and voice chat
  options. See {{page:settings}}.

## 3. Play your first match

A match is a race to the target score, played over several hands:

1. You and Rival each draw a face-down bone for the **hammer**. The higher
   double wins it, and the hammer plays first.
2. You choose your **opening perk** from a draft of cards, then Rival does the
   same.
3. Each hand you pick your seven bones face-down from the shuffled set, then
   take turns playing them onto the table.
4. Whenever the open ends add up to a multiple of five after your play, you
   score that total.
5. The hand ends when someone plays their last bone, or when nobody can play.
   The winner also scores the loser's remaining pips.
6. The loser of each hand drafts another perk, and the next hand begins.

{{page:matches}} explains every step, and {{page:rules}} covers the dominoes
themselves.

## 4. What to do with your first Bone Bucks

Every hand pays Bone Bucks, win or lose. Good first purchases:

- **Unlock perks** in the Perks compendium. Your drafts only offer perks you
  have unlocked, and you start with just three: {{perk:doubles}},
  {{perk:blank}} and {{perk:chain}}. Unlocking {{n:economy.rarityUnlockRequirement}}
  perks of one rarity opens the next rarity for purchase.
- **Spend skill points** as you level. {{skill:end_count}} and
  {{skill:playable_glow}} on the Table Sense tab make the table much easier to
  read.
- **Try a supply** such as a {{supply:table_pass}} from the Supply Shop.

## 5. Keep learning

- Hover any perk, button or bone for a tooltip.
- Open the **Ledger** during a match to see exactly how every score was made.
- **Rival School** (in Tutorials) teaches seven table styles through short
  lessons.
- The {{page:faq}} answers the questions new players ask most.
