# House of Pips Wiki

House of Pips is a domino roguelike. You play Five-Up style dominoes against
Rival, scoring whenever the open ends of the table add up to a multiple of
five, and between hands you draft **perks** that bend the rules in your
favour, alongside the **maluses** that bend them against you.

This wiki explains every rule, perk, supply, skill and mode in House of Pips,
with the same names, rarities and numbers you see on screen.
