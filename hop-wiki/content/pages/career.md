# Career, XP and Bone Bucks

Your **career** is everything that lasts between matches: your level, skills,
Bone Bucks, unlocked perks, supplies, cosmetics and records. The panel at the
top-left of most screens shows your rank, level, XP bar and Bone Bucks.

## XP and levels

You earn career XP from every match:

| Source | XP |
|---|---|
| Each point you score (plays and hand awards) | {{n:career.xp.scorePoint}} |
| Winning a hand | {{n:career.xp.handWin}} |
| Completing a match | {{n:career.xp.matchComplete}} |
| Winning a match | {{n:career.xp.matchWin}} more |

- The XP needed for the next level starts at {{n:career.xpCurveBase}} and grows
  by {{n:career.xpCurveGrowth}} each level. Reaching the level cap of
  {{n:career.levelCap}} takes {{n:career.totalXp}} XP in total.
- **Quick Study** boosts add 25%, and each Prestige rank adds
  {{n:career.prestigeXpPercent}}% more.
- Forfeited matches don't pay the completion bonus, and puzzles don't award
  XP.

## Skill points

Every level you gain gives **one skill point** to spend in the
[Skill tree](skills.html). **Respec** returns every point you spent for
{{n:career.respecCost}} Bone Bucks, or for free with a free respec from the Slap
Track.

## Bone Bucks

Bone Bucks are the currency for unlocking perks and buying supplies, Bone
Boxes and more. Every hand pays out, win or lose:

| Each hand | Bone Bucks |
|---|---|
| Completing the hand | {{n:economy.handRewards.handComplete}} |
| Every {{n:economy.handRewards.pointsPerBuck}} points you banked that hand | 1 |
| Winning the hand | {{n:economy.handRewards.handWin}} more |
| Clean rack (you didn't draw that hand) | {{n:economy.handRewards.cleanHand}} more |
| Comeback hand (you won it while trailing) | {{n:economy.handRewards.comeback}} more |

| Each match | Bone Bucks |
|---|---|
| Completing the match | {{n:economy.matchRewards.matchComplete}} |
| Winning the match | {{n:economy.matchRewards.matchWin}} more |

- **Golden Ledger** boosts add 50%.
- **Last Call** pays a 750 Bone Bucks (and 750 XP) jackpot. See
  {{page:matches}}.
- A forfeited match pays no match reward.

## Unlocking perks

Drafts only offer perks you have **unlocked** in the **Perks** compendium. A
new career starts with {{perk:doubles}}, {{perk:blank}} and {{perk:chain}}.

{{shot:perks-compendium}}

{{perk-prices}}

- **Common** perks can be bought straight away.
- Each higher rarity opens once you own
  {{n:economy.rarityUnlockRequirement}} perks of the rarity below it: five
  Commons open Uncommon, five Uncommons open Rare, five Rares open Epic.
- **Legendary** perks each need three **career trials** completed first. The
  trials appear on the locked card and count your whole career:

{{legendary-trials}}

- Maluses can't be bought. They are always in the draft pool.
- Unlocking is permanent; Prestige does not take unlocked perks away.

## Prestige

Once you reach the level cap and the game offers it, you can **Prestige** to
start your career climb again with permanent bonuses. Each Prestige:

- Adds {{n:career.prestigeBonusPoints}} bonus skill points per Prestige rank
  (they add up across ranks).
- Adds {{n:career.prestigeXpPercent}}% to all the XP you earn, per rank.
- Adds one extra perk card to your drafts (not in All In).
- Puts new cosmetics on the Slap Track.

Prestiging resets your level and XP, skills, Bone Bucks, supplies, queued
boosts, Bone Boxes and Slap Track claims. Unlocked perks, cosmetics, records
and achievement progress all stay. Your rank can go up to 5.

## Records

**Career** on the main menu shows your solo record, online record, playtime and
match history.
