# Online play

**Multiplayer** on the main menu plays one opponent through Steam. The match
is played at the host's table: the host's background, domino skin and music.
Your own volume and mute settings stay your own.

{{shot:multiplayer-entry}}

## What you need

- Steam running, on a Windows PC or a Steam Deck.
- Nana's tutorial completed, like every other mode.
- Both players on the **same version** of the game, or you won't see each
  other's lobbies.
- The demo doesn't include online play.

## Three ways to play

- **Invite a friend** opens a private lobby with voice and typed chat. You
  choose the mode, send a Steam invite, and start the match once your friend
  shows as connected. Only the host can start.
- **Find a match** searches the modes you selected for up to 30 seconds.
- **Quick match** searches every mode for up to 60 seconds.

If nobody is found, the game offers to widen the search to all modes.
Matchmade games pick the host at random and start automatically after a short
countdown, which you can cancel. **Play again** after a match returns you both
to the lobby.

{{shot:multiplayer-lobby}}

## Modes and loadouts

Choose your modes with **Choose game modes**. Each mode card says whether it is
**playable online**. Some modes are solo-only.

Online matches give both players **equal loadouts** instead of your career:

- **Old School**: no skills, perks or supplies, just dominoes.
- **Other modes**: both players play at level 30 with a separate online skill
  build and starting supplies you choose. Use **Build skill loadout** and
  **Choose starting supplies**. Your single-player skills are not affected.
- **Max skills** gives both players every skill at its maximum rank and full
  perk access, first to 250.

Online drafts can offer any perk the mode allows, legendary perks included,
whatever either player has unlocked in single-player.

## Clocks

- Each **turn** has a **60-second** clock. If it runs out, the player who ran
  out of time loses the match.
- **Drafts and hand setup** have **120 seconds**. If time runs out, the game
  makes a default choice for you, such as taking the best perk card offered.
- The clocks pause while a player is reconnecting.

## Disconnects, surrendering and leaving

- If a connection drops, the match waits. Each match allows **two minutes** of
  reconnecting in total, shared across every drop. The screen counts it down.
- If that allowance runs out, the match ends with no win or loss recorded.
- **Surrender** concedes the match: it counts as a loss.
- **Leave match** interrupts it without a win or loss.
- **Rage quit** in Settings concedes the match.

## Rank, level and rewards

Your **online profile** shows a casual rank and an online level from your
finished matches.

- Wins raise your rank; losses and connection losses lower it. Matchmaking
  prefers opponents of a similar rank. Rank tiers run from Boneyard through
  Ivory, Brass and Gold to Delirium.
- Your **online level** only goes up: a win earns more online XP than a loss,
  and interrupted matches earn none.
- Online rewards are **cosmetic only**: tile backs, edge finishes, placement
  effects and badges such as First Table, Ten Wins and Comeback.
- Finishing an online match earns the **Across the Table** achievement.

## Standings and history

{{shot:multiplayer-standings}}

- **Leaderboards** rank players by wins in three groups: public presets,
  private presets and Custom matches. View them worldwide, around your own
  position, or among your friends.
- **History** lists every opponent you've finished a match against.
- You can **block** a player from the lobby or your history. Blocked players
  never appear in your searches and can't invite you.

## Voice chat

Private lobbies include voice chat. The **Voice chat** tab in Settings sets
the chat volume, whether your microphone and your opponent's voice start live
or muted, a voice style, and your speaker and microphone devices. See
{{page:settings}}.
