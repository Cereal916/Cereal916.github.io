# Saving, Continue and Steam Cloud

## Your career saves itself

Your career (level, skills, Bone Bucks, unlocks, supplies, cosmetics and
records) is saved on your computer as you play. There is no manual save.

## Matches save automatically

An unfinished match is saved whenever the table settles after a change, and
closing the window keeps it. Next time, **CONTINUE** on the main menu takes
you back exactly where you left the table.

If you close the game in the middle of a play or on a hand summary, CONTINUE
returns you to the table just before that play. Anything the play already
earned (XP, Bone Bucks, records) stays in your career, and playing it again
doesn't pay it twice.

{{shot:main-menu-continue}}

Puzzles are the exception: an unfinished puzzle starts over when you return
to it.

## Leaving a match

Open **Settings** during a match for two ways out:

- **Save and exit** keeps the match and returns to the menu. It isn't offered
  during cutscenes.
- **Rage quit** forfeits the match (and flips the table): it counts as a loss,
  and you get no match reward.

A friend's multiplayer invitation also offers **Save and join** when the
table or draft is ready to save. It saves the solo match before entering the
lobby, so **Continue** can resume it after multiplayer. If saving fails, the
game keeps your live match and does not join. See {{page:online}}.

Anything that would throw away the current match first asks **Abandon
round?** Choose **Keep playing** to back out with the table exactly as it was.

## Steam Cloud

With Steam Cloud on, your career, unlocks, cosmetics, achievement progress,
online skill build and saved solo match follow you to other computers and
Steam Deck. Display, audio and controller settings stay on each device.

- A saved match only travels to another computer after you leave it with
  **Save and exit**.
- Let Steam finish syncing before you switch computers.
- If the two copies disagree, the game asks you to **choose which progress to
  keep**: **Keep this device**, **Use Steam Cloud**, or **Decide later** and
  keep playing on this device. Each card shows its level, Prestige rank,
  wins and losses, playtime, last played date and saved match, so you can tell
  them apart.
- The copy you choose **replaces the other one**, and that can't be undone.
  Read both cards before you choose.
- The **Steam Cloud** button in the Settings header shows the current sync
  status.

## Clean Slate

**Clean Slate**, in Settings outside a match, **permanently erases** your
career, progress and preferences, including the Steam Cloud copy. It is not a
troubleshooting step. Achievements already unlocked on Steam stay unlocked.
Choose **Keep my account** if you opened it by mistake.

## The demo

The demo saves separately from the full game, doesn't use Steam Cloud, and
doesn't carry its career over to the full game.

## Steam Playtest

Steam Playtest uses the full game's local career and saves, so progress on this
device is shared. Steam Cloud is not enabled for the Playtest.
