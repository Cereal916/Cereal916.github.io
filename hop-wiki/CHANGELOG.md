# House of Pips wiki changes

Changes waiting for the next game release are listed under Unreleased. When the
release is prepared they move under its version, and the site is deployed once
the release ships.

## Unreleased

- First version of the player wiki at `/hop/wiki`: every perk, supply and skill,
  the rules, scoring, modes, career, online play, settings, saving, a glossary
  and an FAQ.
- Describes rules that ship in the next game release: Perk Tax never raises a
  play another malus lowered, Stumble's forced draw ends once the boneyard is
  empty, online drafts can offer Legendary perks, and Continue does not pay a
  replayed play's rewards twice.
- The Spoilers page is hidden.
- Light Fingers keeps the bone with fewer pips for you too, as its card says,
  and sits out a visit that Sovereign Draw, Dead Man's Draw or Greedy Draw
  already changes.
- New Round Insurance and Fresh Cut pictures, matching the game's redrawn
  icons.
- Easier reading: calmer body text and emphasis, more room between list
  items and sections, glossary-style lists with each term on its own line, and
  shorter paragraphs on the busiest pages.
